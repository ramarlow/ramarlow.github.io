[Dropzone Robotics](https://www.dropzonerobotics.com/) builds a wearable on-edge perception system for medical documentation in chaotic and harsh environments. I was brought on to own design and packaging of the hardware on which everything runs, with emphasis on component selection and thermal management through an active cooling system. I worked as the primary designer with input from the company's CEO and from consulting specialists in industrial design and AI, allowing me to bring the first package online in just over two months from starting.

Weight reduction was a major priority for this new system, as the company's original prototype weighed in at more than a kilogram, making it uncomfortable to wear on the head for more than 20 minutes. The biggest weight savings came from designing and a custom forced air heatsink, which I optimized subject to cooling performance, space availability, and manufacturability constraints, then validated at elevated ambient temperatures in a heated chamber. Ultimately I was able to cut the mass down to about 630 grams, for a reduction of over 50%.
